GOT QUESTIONS?

Here's how we get the good times rolling.

Party hire across Melbourne

EPIC Party Hire is a Melbourne party hire company supplying slushie machine hire, popcorn machine hire, fairy floss machines, photo booth hire, giant light-up letters and numbers, karaoke, retro arcade games, outdoor cinema and patio heaters. We set up birthday parties, 18ths and 21sts, kids parties, engagements, weddings, school events and corporate functions across Melbourne and its suburbs.

How do I book EPIC Party Hire? +

Call us on 1800 463 742 or send an enquiry with your preferred date, location and hire items. The crew confirms availability, delivery, inclusions and final pricing, then you can pay by bank transfer to lock in your booking.

Which areas of Melbourne do you deliver to? +

We deliver party hire across Melbourne and surrounding suburbs. Tell us your suburb and we will confirm delivery and any travel costs.

Can I put together my own party hire package? +

Yes. Mix and match slushie machines, popcorn and fairy floss machines, photo booths, light-up letters, karaoke and more, and ask us for a custom quote.

How do I pay for a party package? +

Once we confirm your date and quote, pay by bank transfer using your name and event date as the payment reference. Your booking is locked in once payment is received. See payment details.

Are the promotional prices final? +

Advertised package prices are confirmed together with current availability, delivery and package terms when you enquire.
