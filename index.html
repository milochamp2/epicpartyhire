✳ GOOD TO KNOW

GOT QUESTIONS?

Here's how we get the good times rolling.

How do I book EPIC Party Hire? +

Select a hire item or package and send an enquiry with your preferred date, location and contact details. The crew will follow up to confirm availability, delivery, inclusions and final pricing. Your booking is not confirmed until the team agrees it with you.

Do you deliver and collect? +

Delivery and collection are available subject to location and the hire package. Let us know your suburb so the crew can confirm arrangements and any extra costs.

Can I put together my own package? +

Absolutely. Mix and match your favourites — from photo booths to slushies and light-up letters — and ask us for a custom quote.

Are the promotional prices final? +

Advertised amounts are taken from our promotional artwork. We'll confirm current prices, availability, delivery and package terms when you enquire.
