YOUR DETAILS MATTER

PRIVACY & ENQUIRIES.

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through the enquiry form on this website.

What you enter

The enquiry form collects your name, email address and, if you provide them, your phone number, event date, event type, suburb, preferred hire products and message.

Why it is collected

We use the information to respond to your request, confirm package details and availability, and provide a quote. Sending an enquiry does not create a confirmed booking.

How it is sent

The form emails your details to EPIC Party Hire Melbourne through a secure email delivery provider. You can also choose to send them from your own email app. Please don't include sensitive personal information in your message.

Contact

For questions about your enquiry or to request correction or deletion of information, contact melbourne@epicpartyhire.com.au.
